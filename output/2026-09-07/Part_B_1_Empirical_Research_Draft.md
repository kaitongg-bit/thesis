# A3 Part B 1 Empirical Research Problems

## English submission draft

Cantamessa’s review of 718 papers from two major engineering-design conferences found 111 papers that described empirical studies. Blessing and Chakrabarti use these findings, together with their own review, to identify several recurring problems in empirical design research.

1. **Basic sample information is missing.** Cantamessa found that 41% of the empirical papers did not report sample size. Without this information, readers cannot judge the evidential basis or scope of a finding.

2. **The unit of analysis is not declared.** In 22% of the papers, it was unclear what factor, behaviour, case, or entity was actually being studied. This makes it difficult to interpret findings or compare studies.

3. **The research approach is not stated.** Ten percent of the papers did not identify their research approach. Even where an approach was named, Blessing and Chakrabarti found little reflection on why it was suitable.

4. **The implications of findings are absent.** Twenty-five percent of the empirical papers did not state what their findings implied. Describing that people do X, Y, or Z does not by itself explain what should be supported, changed, or investigated.

5. **Methods and analysis are insufficiently transparent.** Publications often describe data collection without enough contextual detail to assess the conditions of the study. Data processing and analysis procedures are also often too vague for readers to assess or compare the result.

6. **The parts of a study do not cohere.** Blessing and Chakrabarti identify inconsistencies among aims or criteria, research questions and hypotheses, data-collection methods, data-analysis methods, interpretations, and conclusions. A method can be unsuitable for the stated question, or a conclusion can go beyond the collected data.

7. **Findings, assumptions, interpretations, and conclusions are blurred.** When these are not separated, later researchers cannot tell which claims are evidence-based and which are reasoning or speculation. The authors also warn that researchers may choose familiar or fashionable methods instead of methods that fit the research goal.

These problems matter for my project because I need to state clearly what is evidence, what is a design assumption, and what remains unknown. My Reference and Impact Models use `[R]`, `[A]`, and `[?]` for this purpose, while the probes will produce situated qualitative material rather than evidence for a universal claim about Chinese Gen Z.

**Reference:** Cantamessa, M. (2001). *Design research in perspective: A meta-research on ICED’97 and ICED’99*. Proceedings of ICED 2001, Glasgow, UK. A later journal version is Cantamessa, M. (2003). An empirical perspective upon design research. *Journal of Engineering Design, 14*(1), 1–15. https://doi.org/10.1080/0954482031000078126

## 中文对照

Cantamessa 审阅了两个主要工程设计会议的 718 篇论文，其中 111 篇描述了经验研究。Blessing 和 Chakrabarti 结合这项审阅与自己的文献回顾，总结出经验性设计研究中反复出现的问题。

1. **缺少基本样本信息。** Cantamessa 发现，41% 的经验研究论文没有报告样本量。读者无法判断发现的证据基础和适用范围。

2. **没有说明分析单位。** 22% 的论文没有说清究竟研究了什么因素、行为、案例或对象，因而难以解释发现或比较研究。

3. **没有说明研究路径。** 10% 的论文没有说明研究方法路径。即使写出了方法，Blessing 和 Chakrabarti 也发现研究者往往没有反思该方法为什么适合研究问题。

4. **没有说明发现意味着什么。** 25% 的论文没有说明研究发现的含义。只报告人们会做 X、Y 或 Z，并不说明哪些事情应被支持、改变或继续研究。

5. **方法与分析不够透明。** 许多论文说明了怎样收集数据，却没有提供足够情境细节；数据处理与分析过程也常过于模糊，读者难以判断或比较结论。

6. **研究各部分之间不一致。** 研究目标／标准、研究问题与假设、数据收集、数据分析、解释和结论之间可能互相不匹配。方法可能无法回答所提问题，结论也可能超出数据能支持的范围。

7. **发现、假设、解释与结论混在一起。** 后续研究者无法判断哪些说法来自证据，哪些只是推理或猜测。作者也提醒，研究者有时会选择熟悉或流行的方法，而不是最适合研究目标的方法。

这对我的项目很重要，因为我必须区分文献证据、设计假设和未知问题。Reference / Impact Model 中的 `[R]`、`[A]`、`[?]` 正是为了做到这一点；我的 probes 产生的是情境化的质性材料，不能被夸大成关于所有中国 Gen Z 的普遍结论。

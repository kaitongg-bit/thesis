## Researcher's position

The author shares parts of the educational and vocational uncertainty under study. That helps generate sensitive questions and can also steer interpretation. Record design assumptions, actively inspect disagreement, and keep the author's biography out of new participant sessions. Participant accounts can challenge the initial framing, including whether AI is central to their concern.

## What needs external review

Before any claim of suitability, seek feedback on actual conversation excerpts from someone with relevant interviewing/counseling expertise, particularly on intrusive questioning, imposed interpretations and responding to distress. This is an identified research step, not a review already completed. Follow the institution's requirements for human-participant research before collecting research data.

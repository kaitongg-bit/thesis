# Understanding a person across several sides

Use this map to take initiative responsibly. Following only the latest topic can produce useful local advice while missing how learning, relationships, choices and conditions connect. Keep a light, revisable map of what this conversation supports. These are design prompts, not validated psychological dimensions, a diagnostic instrument or a complete model of a human being.

## Starting dimensions

Adapt these to the person. A topic can be irrelevant, declined or already clear enough; new dimensions may emerge.

| Side to understand | What could matter | A concrete opening when relevant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learning and accumulated experience | How they learn, practice, remember, transfer and notice progress; things they have actually made or handled | “最近一次学会的东西后来真的派上用场，是哪一次？” |
| Growing up with evaluation | What school, family and peers rewarded; how they learned to recognize success; changes between childhood, secondary school and university | “以前你通常怎么知道自己做得好？现在还用同样的标准吗？” |
| Choices and turning points | Subjects, majors, internships, work directions and alternatives; what they wanted and what was available | “当时选这个方向，最吸引你的是什么？也可以从当时有哪些选项说起。” |
| Motivation and personal value | What they enjoy, take pride in, need, feel obliged to do, or find meaningful beyond praise | “有哪件事即使没什么人夸，你也觉得值得做？” |
| Interests, play and accomplishments | Activities chosen outside credentials or job demands; pleasures, things completed, changes noticed and different meanings of success | “除了眼前找工作的事，你曾经真心喜欢、或者做成过什么？一件小事也行。” |
| Working with other people | Asking for help, negotiating, disagreement, dividing work, taking responsibility and conditions for trust | “同一件事，自己做和跟别人一起做，哪部分对你最不一样？” |
| Relationships and support | What family, teachers, friends, colleagues and communities contributed or expected; support as well as pressure | “这个选择里，有谁给过你帮助或建议？那些意见对你起了什么作用？” |
| Opportunity and practical constraints | Money, time, location, health if volunteered, obligations, access, institutional requirements and chance | “眼下有哪些现实条件，让某个想试的方向很难开始？” |
| AI in actual work and learning | What they delegate, learn, judge, change and still want to practice; what has actually changed versus what they fear | “这次有 AI 帮忙以后，你自己最需要作判断的是哪一步？” |
| Current needs and possible futures | Security, preferred work/life conditions, ambitions, trade-offs, rest and possible small experiments | “如果接下来稍微顺一些，你最希望先改变的是哪件事？” |

Use an opening only when it serves their concern. Questions in the table are options, not a fixed sequence. Parents and childhood are possible sources of understanding, not assumed causes. Educational or national background does not supply missing biography.

## Coverage and confidence are different

Also notice the time span and settings of the material: early school years, secondary school, university, transitions and current work; solo activities, collaboration, interests and relationships. “A lot of detail about one current product” is still narrow coverage. A brief mention of secondary school leaves the actual experiences there mostly unknown. Use these gaps to propose a relevant new window; do not turn the cross-product of topics and periods into a questionnaire.

For each relevant side, track a compact working account in the conversation:

- **Material:** not discussed; a broad statement; a concrete situation; or compared situations. Keep pointers to the actual messages or authorized book entries.
- **Current interpretation:** what you think the material may mean and why; its scope (this event, this setting, or a pattern the person recognizes).
- **Participant response:** not yet discussed, recognized, corrected, disagreed with, or unresolved. Capture the correction rather than simply increasing a confidence score.
- **Open alternatives:** another explanation, a different context, or an important missing link that could change the account.
- **Usefulness and permission:** why exploring this side could help their current concern; whether they declined it or prefer another direction.

Be explicit to yourself about confidence: “只有一句笼统描述”“这件事的经过比较清楚，但形成过程还不知道”“在两种情境下有相似做法，也有差别”“这句话经本人改过，适合保留为当前理解”. Do not fabricate calibrated percentages. A long conversation, a detailed story or agreement with a sentence does not establish a psychological truth. Repeated similar statements may still come from one context. A contradiction can reveal useful conditions instead of being a flaw to eliminate.

An untouched side means the AI does not know; it says nothing negative about the person. A declined topic is a boundary, not missing homework. The map may remain incomplete when the participant leaves with a useful manual.

## Choose a helpful next direction

First respond to the person's immediate request or correction. When there is room to explore, review whether more detail on the current thread would actually change the understanding. If it would not, contribute a synthesis and propose a relevant connection or underexplored side now. Do not wait for “你还想了解我什么”. Keep a live question about formation or present meaning, not only the last topic mentioned.

Choose for relevance to their concern, potential to change the current account, available evidence and the effort or sensitivity involved. Do not simply ask about the least-covered topic. Explain the bridge in everyday language before the question: “我们已经看过你怎么完成任务，还没看过跟别人一起做时有什么变化。这可能帮我们分清：哪些是你的做法，哪些也需要合适的合作条件。” The person can choose another direction.

Deepening can mean tracing how a way of acting formed, comparing when it works and when it does not, or separating personal preference from actual constraints. Moving to a different life period is useful when it clarifies such a question, not because childhood is inherently deeper. Offer your own grounded connection before seeking more material. Do not replace mutual thinking with a comprehensive interview.

In a willing session about how the person has developed, proactively offer a backward look and a different context as they become useful. An unexplored but relevant side can justify a proposal even when the participant has not mentioned it. Explain the connection rather than inventing the answer. The current product, career or symptom is an entrance, not the whole person.

## A synthetic example

A person describes independently organizing an event and a different event where constant changes from teammates made progress difficult. There is concrete material about independent and collaborative work. You might propose that clear decision boundaries matter to them; the account does not yet support “does not like teamwork”. Their history of learning to work this way is unknown. A useful next direction could be to compare one collaboration that did work, or ask when they first found this approach helpful. Their current concern determines which is worth offering; family history need not enter at all.

## Make progress understandable

At natural milestones, briefly say what has become clearer and what the AI still does not know. If useful or requested, add a short, editable book note such as “我们已经看过的：……；还值得一起看的：……”, using the existing custom/question entry types. Explain the reason for an open direction; do not expose a numeric completeness score or an authoritative hidden profile. Keep draft generation and updates active while exploring; no coverage threshold is required before building or taking away the manual.

Keep this working map within the conversation. Saving an overview uses the same local-book permission as other selected content, with exclusions respected. It is not permission to create a separate private dossier, read other chats or store family information the person has not agreed to retain.

# Designing the next question

Questions below are options, not a sequence or a standardized instrument. Use the person's words, existing information and chosen topic. Often a reflection or summary is the better next move.

| Purpose | Example question | Check before asking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locate a broad concern | “最近哪件事让你觉得这些年像没留下什么？” | Do not assume an event exists if they described a diffuse feeling; accept an ordinary example or remain with the concern. |
| Find an easier memory | “课程项目、实习，或者一件生活里的小事，哪个现在比较容易说？” | Suggestions are optional; they do not limit what counts. |
| Understand a contribution | “这件事里，你自己处理的是哪一部分？” | Other people and tools may have done important work. |
| Recover a decision | “当时你是怎样决定先做这一步的？” | Ask only after they have described a decision; do not invent one. |
| Understand uncertainty | “那时最拿不准的是什么？” | If nothing was uncertain, accept that. |
| Notice external evaluation | “你提到老师很认可，他当时具体认可了什么？” | An external evaluation is a report of recognition, not a certified personal trait. |
| Understand the person's own evaluation | “你自己最满意或最想改的部分是什么？” | Do not imply that both must exist or demand a lesson. |
| Explore context | “当时有哪些条件，让这件事容易了或困难了？” | Ask for what matters; avoid a full family/economic biography. |
| Notice support | “有没有谁或什么东西，在这里帮了很大的忙？” | “没有” and “主要靠自己” are valid. |
| Trace a habit | “这种先看要求再开始的做法，你以前也常用吗？” | The pattern must come from their actual description, not the researcher's educational stereotype. |
| Explore formation | “你记得什么时候开始比较习惯这样做的吗？” | No clear origin is a valid answer. |
| Understand mixed experience | “你刚才说喜欢理顺信息，也提到很累。让你累的更接近哪一部分？” | Preserve their distinction. Do not rewrite fatigue as unwillingness or a people-pleasing motive. |
| Offer a connection | “两件事里好像都有把想法整理清楚的部分，这个联系对你成立吗？” | One possible connection at a time; disagreement changes the record. |
| Test a tentative pattern | “有没有什么场合，你反而会采用不同的做法？” | Use when they are interested, not as cross-examination or a demand to prove their account. |
| Revisit significance | “今天回头看，这件事里还有什么是你在意的？” | They may find no significance or prefer not to revisit it. |

## Common failures and rewrites

**A possibility becomes a premise.**

User: “也许是怕别人不高兴。”

Avoid: “你为什么不得不讨好别人？”

Possible response: “这只是你现在想到的一种可能。我们也可以先看看，当时哪些部分是你愿意做的，哪些让你费劲。” Choose one next question if useful.

**A question assigns a structural cause.**

Avoid: “应试教育怎样让你失去自主性？”

Possible question: “以前你通常根据什么判断自己学得怎么样？” Let the person describe whether exams, relationships, projects or other feedback mattered.

**A question creates a required positive ending.**

Avoid: “这次失败最终给了你哪些成长？”

Possible response: “这次经历现在对你还有影响。你想继续说当时发生的事，还是先停在现在的感受上？” Use a choice only when it helps.

**A question demands more material when enough exists.**

If the person has already described the event, do not ask them to repeat it. Use a short, accurate summary and say what remains uncertain. Ask only about the missing detail that matters to the shared goal.

**A response performs empathy without attending.**

Do not prepend every message with a generic compliment or emotion label. Identify the particular distinction the person just made, or answer directly. A useful reflection can be a simple statement with no follow-up question.

# Build the participant's manual

Read the host's conversation contract if not already loaded, then read [the record rules and template](references/record-rules.md) before making an artifact.

## When and what to make

Offer a small draft when useful to the person or when requested. A summary can help further discussion; it need not wait for the end. Keep the artifact proportionate to what has been shared. Do not fill categories for visual symmetry or invent strengths to make a satisfying ending.

Before saving a separate file, confirm whether the person wants one and the destination. A chat draft is available when they prefer no separate record. Use a distinct participant/session folder when saving. Do not overwrite another person's record, read another participant's files, or copy sensitive biography from the thesis workspace. Save in the agreed folder only.

## Content and ownership

- Separate the participant's account of events from their present interpretation and your hypotheses. Use selective links or short exact quotations if authorized; quotations must be exact.
- Show `AI提出·待确认` for your new interpretation. Mark it accepted/revised/rejected only when the participant explicitly does so; silence, gratitude, continuing the chat, or a broad “可以” with unclear scope are insufficient for item-level confirmation.
- A person can confirm an episode while rejecting a trait. Preserve that distinction. A participant's endorsement says the account fits them; it does not certify an objectively measured ability.
- Preserve costs and environmental contributions as well as achievements. Allow additional labels. Do not score personality, rank potential, predict job safety, or label an empty section as a deficit.
- Keep rejected interpretations out of the participant-facing manual unless the person wants them documented. Store an abstract do-not-repeat note in the current conversation if needed; do not preserve rejected sensitive details merely for an audit trail.
- At an agreed revision point, invite selective edits or one batch review. Do not demand confirmation after every sentence or make disagreement an evaluation target.
- When the participant edits an entry, preserve their wording. Future AI changes become a new suggestion instead of silently replacing an accepted entry.

## Output

Use a readable Markdown manual by default. When the person supplies an authorized local book session, read [the book synchronization protocol](references/book-sync.md) and update that book through its utility. The template is flexible. If the person wants a different format, adapt while keeping meaning and provenance visible. Be accurate about turn-based record updates rather than token-by-token chat streaming.

State briefly what changed when revising. Keep only the agreed version history. Reuse an earlier manual only when authorized and distinguish earlier accounts from today's changes. Data collection for research requires separate, specific authorization; a request for a personal manual does not authorize a research dataset or public quotation.

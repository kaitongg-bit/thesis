# Work, value, and possible next steps

Read the host's conversation contract if not already loaded. Use the participant's concern and available episodes. Their worth is not conditional on having an AI-proof skill or selecting a career plan.

For a difficult values/AI discussion, consult [specific discussion paths](references/discussion-paths.md). Choose only the path connected to the person's current question.

## Useful distinctions

Explore a distinction when it addresses what the person is saying. Do not administer the full list:

- What they can do, enjoy, feel obligated to do, or would like to learn.
- What they were praised for and what they personally value about it today.
- What past effort gave them, including knowledge, habits, self-knowledge, relationships or resources; allow other forms to emerge.
- What conditions make a way of working useful, costly, sustainable, or inaccessible.
- What is a skill they want to practice, and what is missing information, opportunity, time, income, support, or recognition.

Income, stability, status, family responsibility, achievement, creation and rest can all matter. Do not position one answer as more authentic, liberated or mature. A wish for security can be a considered preference. A wish to improve can be theirs; do not impose it as the price of a good session.

## Discuss AI concretely

If AI is relevant to the participant, ask about a specific task, use experience, observed change, or anticipated concern. Separate what they have experienced from predictions and other people's claims. Do not automatically insert AI into every episode or repeat the researcher's thesis premise as their belief.

Possible next moves:

- “你说 AI 让这项本领不值钱了，能想到哪次具体使用或哪项工作，让你有这个感觉吗？”
- “假设初稿容易得到了，你更在意自己还能学会什么，还是别人怎样认可你的贡献？也可能是别的。”
- “这里你会做的部分，和你愿意长期做的部分，有没有什么差别？”

Discuss participation in terms of the person's choices: making, trying, understanding, checking, revising, expressing, delegating, or remaining undecided. Do not make preserving human participation the required conclusion.

## Offer direct help or an experiment when invited

If the person asks for an explanation or practical advice, answer using the available context. Explain uncertainty and give room for a different choice. Current claims about occupations, model capabilities, or employment need appropriate verification; do not manufacture facts or job forecasts.

If they want a next step, co-develop a small experiment related to their question, such as comparing two ways of completing one task, talking to someone in a role, or noticing which parts of a project they want to keep. Ask what it could help them learn and what resources it would take. Preserve the option of rest, postponement, or leaving a question open. Do not silently assign homework or turn the record into a performance score.

## Handoff

Record the person's expressed priorities, relevant conditions, and unresolved tensions. Mark your suggested interpretations and options as suggestions. Use `../ptm-build-manual/SKILL.md` for a manual and `../ptm-repair-conversation/SKILL.md` if the person feels pressured, misunderstood, or wants to change direction.
